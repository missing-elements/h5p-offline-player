H5P to SCORM

Choose .h5p files, or drop them on this page, check what each zip will hold, and download them as SCORM packages for your LMS. Nothing is uploaded: the packages are built here, in your browser, with the player this site serves.

What each zip will hold, read from the package before anything is built. The title is what the LMS lists the package by; change it here if you like.

    3 Options

    For every zip. Left as they are, a zip is SCORM 1.2 and takes the content's own result, as this page has always written it.

    SCORM version

    A score at or above it passes, whatever the content says, and the LMS gets it as the mastery score. Content with no score gets no pass or fail from it.

    For a gradebook that needs a number: a questionnaire, submitted, records 100. Content that never finishes, such as an Accordion or a presentation without questions, stays incomplete.

    In the LMS

    Add the zip to a course as a SCORM activity. The LMS records the score out of 100, whether the content was passed or failed, or completed when it does not say, each question's answer, the time spent, and the learner's place, so the next launch continues where they left off. Content that reports no result of its own, an Accordion or a Virtual Tour, stays incomplete.

    What goes in the zip

    The package, the player and the H5P runtime, and a launch page that reports to the LMS. A package exported without its libraries, as h5p.com and h5p.org export, gets the ones it needs from this site's copy of the library pack, and only those; one that carries its libraries plays with its own. A package that needs a library neither has is refused here, with the library named, rather than in front of a learner.
