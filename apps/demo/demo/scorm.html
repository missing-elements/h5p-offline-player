SCORM

H5P to SCORM

Drop a .h5p file here, or choose one, and download it as a SCORM 1.2 package for your LMS. It is built in your browser with the player this site serves, and nothing is uploaded: the package never leaves your computer.

In the LMS

Add the zip to a course as a SCORM activity. The LMS records the score out of 100, whether the content was passed or failed, or completed when it does not say, the time spent, and the learner's place, so the next launch continues where they left off. Content that reports no result of its own, an Accordion or a Virtual Tour, stays incomplete. The package plays with its own code on the LMS's origin, as every SCORM package does.

Another LMS that takes SCORM 1.2 may work as well; these are the ones checked, as a learner in Chrome. The LMS has to be served over https, since the player needs its Service Worker. SCORM 1.2 keeps 4,096 characters of saved state, enough for a quiz; a larger state is not saved.

What goes in the zip

The package, the player and the H5P runtime, and a launch page that reports to the LMS. A package exported without its libraries, as h5p.com and h5p.org export, gets the ones it needs from this site's copy of the library pack, and only those: a quiz with formulas takes eleven, most of it the formula renderer. One that carries its libraries, as exports from Lumi and the H5P plugins of Moodle, WordPress and Drupal do, plays with its own. A package that needs a library neither has is refused here, with the library named, rather than in front of a learner.

From the command line

The same zip, from the same code, for a script or a pipeline; on Node 22 or later. A site written by h5p-embed for a player domain of your own carries this page too, at /scorm.html. 

npx @missing-elements/h5p-embed@latest --scorm course.h5p     # writes ./course.scorm.zip
