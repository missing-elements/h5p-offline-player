Paste a URL to a .h5p file, or pick one from disk. Nothing is unpacked on a server — the archive is read in place and the files are served to the H5P runtime by a Service Worker. Need it offline? Install the H5P Offline Player app — a PWA for Chrome and Edge that opens .h5p files from your computer and plays them with no network.
